## Post

Who is this page for? Most teams answer that before they write a word, and then the page can only ever serve one audience.

In DocStar the order is reversed. You write the document first, together with your team, and it is structured and versioned from the start. Who reads it is decided when you publish: internal docs, product docs, help docs, API docs or a blog.

A few things follow from that:

- A rough team note can be promoted to a public help page without being rewritten in another tool.
- Edits stay in one place, so the published page does not quietly drift from the original.
- Internal and public writing live side by side, so people stop maintaining two systems.

It is a small change in order, but it removes a lot of copy and paste.

See how it works: https://docstar.io
